# Institutional platform release — 22 November 2026

This release extends the existing IPPAN application, dedicated Neon database, session authentication and role checks. Existing records are retained. Additive JSON document upgrades initialize CMS and meeting collections without destructive schema changes.

Implemented:

- Institutional homepage in the supplied section order; grouped navigation, public search, AGM information, media gallery and newsletter preferences.
- English/Nepali homepage, navigation and institutional pages, bilingual editorial fields, a Devanagari web font and Unicode PDF font support. Editorial records without a Nepali edition fall back to English; not every administrative help paragraph has a translation.
- One filtered hydropower dataset for KPIs, six charts, table, CSV export and a geographic country-outline map. Operational capacity is separated from registered portfolio capacity. Project coordinates remain unverified demonstration fixtures; country geometry is attributed to Natural Earth.
- CMS page draft/review/publish/archive, SEO metadata, About navigation entries, banner content, leadership biographies, photo uploads and video links. Publishing a page updates its public route and metadata.
- Corporate, associate and individual categories, configurable fees/documents, member record editing, distribution charts and renewal/expiry reporting.
- Operational dashboard from recorded dates and transactions, work queues, event statistics and tasks. Detailed membership, finance, hydropower and event reports include date filters, CSV and print/PDF output.
- Configurable event ticket categories with server-enforced prices and capacity; existing registration, QR passes and attendance are retained.
- Private correspondence attachments and review fields; meetings with agenda, confirmed attendance, minutes, documents and assigned decisions. Existing Darta/Chalani references are retained.
- Staff creation, role assignment, server-backed permission matrix, audit view, institutional settings, contact inquiry inbox, communication templates/drafts and private notification preferences. Planned communication dates are records for manual follow-up, not an automatic delivery scheduler.
- CSV with quoted values and Excel `.xlsx` project imports, with complete batch validation before persistence.

Important routes: `/`, `/about`, `/hydropower`, `/members`, `/membership`, `/resources`, `/publications`, `/news`, `/events`, `/agm`, `/media`, `/search`, `/pages/member-services`, `/admin/website`, `/admin/categories`, `/admin/renewals`, `/admin/meetings`, `/admin/secretariat`, `/admin/reports`, `/admin/audit`, `/admin/settings`, and the existing `/portal` workflows.

Verification: automated domain, analytics, privacy, ticket and document-font tests; production build/TypeScript; lint with retained HTML-image optimization warnings; existing 34 live HTTP assertions against dedicated Neon; browser CMS publishing, Nepali/mobile layout, synchronized filters/map, Excel validation, meeting PDF upload; guest/member denial from private meeting documents. Exact final check results and deployment provenance are recorded in DEPLOYMENT.md after release.

Boundaries: approved officer identities, institutional source documents and authoritative national project/statistical data still need IPPAN input. Formal fiscal-year numbering and institutional election rules require approval. Payments and voting are explicitly demonstrations. Native applications and real payment/email/SMS/push integrations remain excluded or credential-dependent. PDF documents use a native accessible browser reader; no page-turn flipbook is claimed. This is not a claim of full TOR certification or production election/security readiness.
